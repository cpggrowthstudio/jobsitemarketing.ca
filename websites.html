Questions

How long until it is live?

About two weeks from the day you send me your photos and details. The Google profile is usually sorted in the first few days, before the site is even finished.

How long until I show up on Google?

The map usually starts moving in two to four weeks. Search rankings take longer, two to three months is normal for a new site. Anyone who promises you faster than that is guessing.

I already have a website.

Then we look at whether it is worth keeping. Sometimes the problem is not the site, it is that the Google profile was never filled out and there are four reviews on it. I will tell you if you do not need a new site.

What do you need from me?

Your logo if you have one, about ten photos off your phone, your number and the towns you cover. Thirty minutes in total, and I do the rest.

Yes. Cancel whenever and I hand over the files and help you move the hosting wherever you want. No hostage taking.
