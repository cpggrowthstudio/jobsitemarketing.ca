Questions people ask.

Does it sound like a robot?

No. It talks like a person, pauses when the caller talks and handles interruptions. If someone cuts in with "wait, do you do diesels?" it answers and picks the thread back up. You choose the voice.

Will it quote jobs over the phone?

It gives a ballpark range you set, like "repairs usually run a few hundred to fifteen hundred." It never gives a firm price on a job it has not seen, because that is how you end up eating a bad number. It books you to go look.

What if I want to answer my own phone?

You do. It only picks up when you do not, after however many rings you choose. Or set it to after hours only. It is there for the calls you would otherwise lose, not the ones you would take.

Do I need a new phone number?

No. We forward your existing number so nothing changes for your customers or your truck decals.

How long does setup take?

Usually within a week. You tell us your services, your hours and a rough price range for each. We do the rest and test it with a few calls before it goes live.
