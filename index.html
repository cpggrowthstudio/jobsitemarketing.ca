Questions

Before you start.

What happens if they text instead of call?

It texts back and has a proper conversation. Not an auto reply saying sorry we missed you, an actual back and forth that gets the address, gives your price range and books the job. Plenty of people would rather text than talk, and that is most of the point.

Will it quote jobs over the phone?

It gives a rough range you set, like "repairs usually run a few hundred to fifteen hundred." It never gives a firm price on a job it has not seen, because that is how you end up eating a bad number. It books you to go and look.

Do I need a new phone number?

No. We forward your existing number, so nothing changes for your customers or your truck decals.
