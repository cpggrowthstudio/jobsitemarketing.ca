Questions

Before you start.

Is this just missed call text back?

No. You get that too, as a backstop, but it is not the point. A text back only tells someone you missed them, and by then they have called the next company on Google. Ours answers the call and talks to them. That is the difference.

Does the AI sound like a robot?

It talks like a person, pauses when the caller talks and handles interruptions. You choose the voice. If it sounds wrong to you, it is a no, and we would not sell it to you anyway.

Will it quote jobs over the phone?

It gives a rough range you set, like "repairs usually run a few hundred to fifteen hundred." It never gives a firm price on a job it has not seen, because that is how you end up eating a bad number. It books you to go and look.

Do I need a new phone number?

No. We forward your existing number, so nothing changes for your customers or your truck decals.

What if I want to answer my own phone?

You do. It only picks up when you do not, after however many rings you choose. Or set it to after hours only. It is there for the calls you would otherwise lose, not the ones you would take.

How long does setup take?

About two weeks. You fill in one form about your services, your hours and a rough price range for each. We build it, test it, and it does not go near a customer until you are happy.

Am I locked in?

No. Month to month, 30 days notice whenever you want. And 30 day money back if it is not for you.
